We do not sell personal information. We share it only with:

| Recipient | Purpose | Safeguards |
|-----------|---------|------------|
| Payment processors (Stripe) | Payment processing | PCI-DSS certified; tokenised data only |

| Analytics tools (self-hosted, no third-party analytics) | Website analytics | No personal data sent to third parties |
| Government authorities | Legal compliance (tax, food safety recalls) | Only when required by law |

## 5. Data Residency

All personal information is stored and processed in **Canada**, in the AWS
ca-central-1 region (Montréal). We do not transfer personal information outside
Canada unless required by law or with your explicit consent.

## 6. Retention Periods

| Data type | Retention period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Account information | Duration of the account + 3 years |
| Transaction history | 7 years (tax and accounting obligations) |
| Loyalty program data | Duration of membership + 1 year |
| Support tickets | 3 years from resolution |
| AI-derived scores | Recalculated quarterly; historical scores retained for 1 year |
| Behavioural data (cookies, clicks) | 13 months |
| Payment tokens | Duration of the account; full card numbers are never stored |

Data is deleted or anonymised at the end of the retention period.

## 7. Automated Decision-Making

Boréal Marché uses automated systems to make or assist the following decisions:

### 7.1 Retention prediction (Head A)

A machine-learning model predicts the probability that a customer will place
another order in the next 90 days. This score is used to:

- Select which customers receive a retention coupon.
- Prioritise customer-service outreach.

**This is an automated decision that can affect the offers you receive.** Under
Law 25, you have the right to be informed that such a decision has been made, to
request the factors that contributed to the decision, and to have the decision
reviewed by a human. See Section 8.

### 7.2 Product recommendations (Head B)

An association-rule engine suggests products based on the contents of your
shopping basket. This is a convenience feature and does not restrict your access
to any product. The recommendations are based on aggregated, de-identified
purchase patterns, not on your individual profile.

### 7.3 Customer assistant (Head C)

The bilingual assistant uses retrieval-augmented generation to answer questions
about our policies. It calls our internal tools (retention score, recommendations,
product lookup) but **never discloses another customer's data** and **never
provides medical, legal, or financial advice**.

## 8. Your Rights

Under Law 25 and PIPEDA, you have the right to:

1. **Access** your personal information held by Boréal Marché.
2. **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete information.
3. **Withdraw consent** for non-essential processing (e.g., promotional emails,
   loyalty program, personalised recommendations).
4. **Request deletion** of your account and associated data, subject to legal
   retention obligations.
5. **Data portability** — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
6. **Be informed** of any automated decision that affects you and the principal
   factors involved.
7. **Request a human review** of an automated decision.
8. **File a complaint** with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec
   (CAI) if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Privacy Officer at
privacy@borealmarche.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

## 10. Security

We protect your personal information with:

- TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit.
- AES-256 encryption for data at rest.
- Multi-factor authentication for employee access to personal data.
- Annual penetration testing by an independent security firm.
- Access logging and monitoring with automated alerts for anomalies.
