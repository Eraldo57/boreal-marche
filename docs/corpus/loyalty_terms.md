# Boréal Marché — Programme Étoiles Boréales (Loyalty Program Terms)

*Last updated: 2024-11-01*

## 1. Overview

The Étoiles Boréales loyalty program rewards Boréal Marché customers with
points for every purchase. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future
orders or for exclusive products.

Membership is free and open to all customers with a registered Boréal Marché
account. There is no annual fee.

## 2. Earning Points

### 2.1 Base earning rate

Members earn **1 point per $1 spent** (before taxes, after discounts). Shipping
fees do not earn points.

### 2.2 Bonus categories

Certain product categories earn bonus points:

| Category | Earning rate |
|----------|-------------|
| Cuisine Boréal (prepared meals) | 2× (2 points per $1) |
| Organic products (tagged 🍃) | 1.5× (1.5 points per $1) |
| Boréal Box subscription | 2× (2 points per $1) |
| Wine and spirits | 1× (standard rate) |
| Gift cards | 0× (no points) |

### 2.3 Welcome bonus

New members receive **200 bonus points** upon placing their first order of $50 or
more.

### 2.4 Birthday bonus

Members who have provided their date of birth receive **100 bonus points** on
their birthday month, credited on the first day of the month. The birthday
bonus must be redeemed within 60 days.

### 2.5 Referral bonus

When a member refers a new customer using their unique referral code, both the
referring member and the new customer receive **150 bonus points** after the new
customer's first order of $50 or more is delivered.

### 2.6 Review bonus

Members earn **10 points** for each product review (text review of at least 50
characters), up to a maximum of 10 reviews per calendar month.

## 3. Tiers

The program has three tiers based on cumulative spending in a rolling 12-month
period:

| Tier | Annual spend | Perks |
|------|-------------|-------|
| 🌱 Pousse (Seedling) | $0 – $499 | Base earning rate |
| 🌲 Sapin (Fir) | $500 – $1,499 | 1.25× earning multiplier; free standard shipping on orders over $50; early access to seasonal products |
| ❄️ Étoile (Star) | $1,500+ | 1.5× earning multiplier; free express shipping on all orders; dedicated support line; annual 10% birthday coupon; invitation to tasting events |

Tier status is evaluated on the first day of each month. If a member's rolling
12-month spend drops below a tier's threshold, they are moved to the appropriate
lower tier. A one-month grace period applies.

## 4. Redeeming Points

### 4.1 Discount redemption

Points can be redeemed for a discount on an order at checkout:

| Points | Discount |
|--------|----------|
| 500 | $5.00 |
| 1,000 | $10.00 |
| 2,500 | $25.00 |
| 5,000 | $55.00 |

Redemption is available in increments of 500 points. Points and dollar discounts
cannot be combined on the same order with other promotional codes unless the
promotion explicitly allows it.

### 4.2 Exclusive products

From time to time, Boréal Marché offers exclusive products redeemable only with
points (e.g., limited-edition gift baskets, branded merchandise). These products
are listed under "Boutique Étoiles" on the website.

### 4.3 Charitable donations

Members can convert points into donations to partnered food banks:

- 500 points = $5 donation to Moisson Montréal
- 500 points = $5 donation to Banques alimentaires du Québec

## 5. Point Expiry

- Points earned through purchases expire **24 months** after the date they were
  earned.
- Bonus points (welcome, birthday, referral, review) expire **12 months** after
  the date they were credited.
- When points are redeemed, the oldest points are used first (FIFO).
- Members receive an email notification 30 days before any points are due to
  expire.

## 6. Account Rules

- Points are non-transferable between accounts.
- Points have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- A member may hold only one loyalty account. Duplicate accounts will be merged;
  the point balances are combined but duplicate bonus credits are removed.
- Boréal Marché reserves the right to cancel points earned through fraudulent
  activity (e.g., fake reviews, self-referrals, returning items after redeeming
  points on the same order).

## 7. Returns and Cancellations

- If an order is returned or cancelled, the points earned on that order are
  deducted from the member's balance.
- If a member redeemed points on an order that is later returned, the points are
  restored and the refund is reduced by the discount amount.

## 8. Program Changes

Boréal Marché may modify the program terms, earning rates, redemption values, or
tier thresholds with 30 days' notice. Notice is provided by email and on the
website. Existing point balances remain valid under the new terms.

## 9. Termination

Boréal Marché may terminate a member's loyalty account for violation of these
terms or for fraudulent activity. Upon termination, all accumulated points are
forfeited.

A member may voluntarily close their loyalty account by contacting
fidelite@borealmarche.ca. Unredeemed points are forfeited upon closure.
