# Boréal Marché — Delivery Policy

*Last updated: 2024-11-01*

## 1. Service Area

Boréal Marché delivers to residential and commercial addresses in the following
zones:

| Zone | Provinces covered | Standard delivery | Express delivery |
|------|-------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| Zone 1 — Montréal métropolitain | Montréal, Laval, Longueuil, Brossard, Repentigny | Next-day | Same-day (order before 11 h) |
| Zone 2 — Québec élargi | Québec City, Gatineau, Sherbrooke, Trois-Rivières, Saguenay, and all other QC municipalities | 2–3 business days | Next-day |
| Zone 3 — Ontario | Toronto, Ottawa, Hamilton, and all ON postal codes | 3–5 business days | 2 business days |
| Zone 4 — Maritimes | New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island | 5–7 business days | 3–4 business days |

We do not currently deliver to Newfoundland and Labrador, Manitoba, Saskatchewan,
Alberta, British Columbia, or the Territories. Orders placed with a shipping
address outside our service area will be cancelled and refunded automatically.

## 2. Delivery Windows

### 2.1 Standard Delivery

Standard delivery is available for all orders. Delivery windows are assigned
based on the zone and the time the order is confirmed:

- **Orders confirmed before 14 h (Eastern)** are dispatched the same business day.
- **Orders confirmed after 14 h** are dispatched the next business day.
- Saturday orders are dispatched on Monday.
- Statutory holidays (federal and provincial) are non-dispatch days.

Customers receive an email with a tracking number once the order is dispatched.
A two-hour delivery window is communicated by SMS on the morning of the delivery
day.

### 2.2 Express Delivery

Express delivery is available in Zones 1 and 2 for an additional fee. In Zone 1,
same-day delivery is offered for orders placed and paid before 11 h Eastern. In
Zone 2, express orders are delivered next business day.

Express delivery is not available for orders exceeding 30 kg total weight or for
orders containing oversized items (items marked "LTL" in the catalogue).

### 2.3 Click-and-Collect

Free pickup is available at our Montréal warehouse (4300 rue Saint-Ambroise,
suite 200) and our Québec City depot (1150 boulevard Charest Ouest). Orders are
ready within 4 hours of confirmation. Uncollected orders are held for 48 hours
and then cancelled with a full refund.

## 3. Shipping Fees

| Zone | Standard fee | Express fee | Free-shipping threshold |
|------|-------------|-------------|------------------------|
| Zone 1 | $6.95 | $12.95 | $75.00 |
| Zone 2 | $9.95 | $17.95 | $100.00 |
| Zone 3 | $14.95 | $24.95 | $150.00 |
| Zone 4 | $19.95 | $34.95 | $200.00 |

Shipping fees are calculated per order, not per item. The free-shipping threshold
applies to the subtotal before taxes. Promotional codes that reduce the subtotal
may cause shipping fees to apply even if the original subtotal exceeded the
threshold.

## 4. Cold-Chain and Temperature-Sensitive Products

### 4.1 Refrigerated items

Products tagged as "refrigerated" (dairy, deli meats, fresh juices, prepared
meals) are shipped in insulated packaging with gel ice packs rated for the
transit duration of the delivery zone. The packaging maintains a temperature
between 0 °C and 4 °C for up to 48 hours.

### 4.2 Frozen items

Products tagged as "frozen" (frozen fruits, ice cream, frozen meals) are shipped
in insulated containers with dry ice. Frozen shipments are only available for
Zones 1 and 2. Orders containing frozen items that are shipped to Zone 3 or
Zone 4 will have those items removed and refunded. The customer is notified by
email before dispatch.

### 4.3 Ambient items

Products that do not require temperature control (canned goods, dry pasta,
snacks, cleaning supplies) are shipped in standard cardboard boxes.

### 4.4 Mixed Orders

An order can contain refrigerated, frozen, and ambient items. The order is
packed in separate containers per temperature class. All containers ship together
in one delivery.

## 5. Delivery Issues

### 5.1 Late delivery

If a standard delivery arrives more than two business days after the estimated
delivery date, the shipping fee is automatically refunded to the original payment
method. No claim is required.

### 5.2 Damaged or missing items

Customers must report damaged or missing items within 48 hours of delivery
by contacting support@borealmarche.ca or by using the "Report an issue" button
in the order history page. A photo of the damaged item is required for damage
claims. Approved claims result in a refund or a replacement at the customer's
choice.

### 5.3 Failed delivery

If a delivery cannot be completed (no one available, wrong address, access
issues), the driver will make one additional attempt the next business day. If
the second attempt fails, the order is returned to our warehouse. Perishable
items cannot be re-delivered and are refunded. Non-perishable items can be
re-delivered for a fee of $9.95 or picked up at the nearest depot.
