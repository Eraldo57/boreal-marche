# Boréal Marché — Allergen and Food Safety Policy

*Last updated: 2024-11-01*

## 1. Commitment

Boréal Marché is committed to the safety of all customers, including those with
food allergies, intolerances, and celiac disease. We follow the allergen
labelling requirements of the *Food and Drugs Act* (Canada) and the
*Règlement sur les aliments* (Québec).

## 2. Priority Allergens

Health Canada identifies the following priority allergens. All Boréal Marché
products must declare the presence of these allergens on the product page and
on the physical label:

1. Peanuts
2. Tree nuts (almonds, Brazil nuts, cashews, hazelnuts, macadamia nuts, pecans,
   pine nuts, pistachios, walnuts)
3. Sesame seeds
4. Milk
5. Eggs
6. Fish (all species)
7. Crustaceans and shellfish
8. Soy
9. Wheat and triticale
10. Mustard
11. Sulphites (at 10 ppm or more)
12. Gluten sources (wheat, rye, barley, oats — unless certified gluten-free)

## 3. Allergen Labelling on the Website

### 3.1 Product pages

Every product page on borealmarche.ca includes:

- **Ingredient list** — reproduced verbatim from the manufacturer's label.
- **Contains** — a highlighted box listing all priority allergens present in the
  product (e.g., "Contains: milk, soy, wheat").
- **May contain** — a highlighted box listing allergens that may be present due
  to cross-contamination during manufacturing (e.g., "May contain traces of
  peanuts and tree nuts").

### 3.2 Filters

Customers can filter search results by allergen:

- "Peanut-free"
- "Nut-free"
- "Gluten-free"
- "Dairy-free"
- "Egg-free"
- "Vegan" (excludes all animal-derived allergens)

These filters exclude products that **contain** or **may contain** the selected
allergen. However, Boréal Marché **cannot guarantee** that filtered results are
safe for individuals with severe allergies, because allergen information depends
on data provided by manufacturers and may change without notice. Always read the
physical label before consuming.

### 3.3 Product changes

Manufacturers sometimes reformulate products, adding or removing allergens. When
Boréal Marché is notified of a reformulation, the product page is updated within
5 business days. We encourage customers to check the physical label each time
they purchase, as the website may not yet reflect the latest formulation.

## 4. Cross-Contamination in Our Facilities

### 4.1 Warehouse handling

Our Montréal and Québec City warehouses handle products containing all priority
allergens. Products are stored by category (dairy, bakery, dry goods) but not in
allergen-isolated zones. Therefore:

- **We cannot certify that any product handled in our warehouse is free from
  cross-contact with any allergen.**
- Products with intact, sealed manufacturer packaging are at minimal risk of
  cross-contamination in our facility.

### 4.2 Boréal Marché prepared meals

Our in-house prepared meals ("Cuisine Boréal") are produced in a kitchen that
processes milk, eggs, wheat, soy, fish, and tree nuts. All Cuisine Boréal
products carry a "may contain" advisory for these allergens even if they are not
intentional ingredients.

Cuisine Boréal does **not** use peanuts in any recipe. However, peanut-containing
products are stored elsewhere in the same warehouse, so a "may contain peanuts"
advisory applies.

## 5. Recalls

### 5.1 Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA) recalls

When the CFIA issues a recall for a product sold on Boréal Marché:

1. The product is immediately removed from the website and from warehouse
   inventory.
2. All customers who purchased the recalled product within the recall period
   receive an email and an SMS notification within 12 hours.
3. The notification includes the recall reason, the affected lot numbers, and
   instructions (typically: do not consume, discard or return for a full refund).
4. A full refund is issued automatically — no action is required from the
   customer.

### 5.2 Voluntary recalls by manufacturers

Boréal Marché cooperates with manufacturers who initiate voluntary recalls. The
same notification process applies.

### 5.3 Internal quality alerts

If our quality team identifies a potential allergen mislabelling or contamination
issue before a CFIA recall is issued, we proactively remove the product and
notify affected customers. We then report the issue to the CFIA as required by
the *Safe Food for Canadians Act*.

## 6. Special Diets

Boréal Marché provides informational tags to help customers following special
diets:

| Tag | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| 🌿 Vegan | Contains no animal-derived ingredients; may be produced in a facility that processes animal products |
| 🥛 Lactose-free | No lactose; may still contain milk proteins |
| 🌾 Gluten-free | Certified gluten-free (< 20 ppm) by a recognised body |
| 🥜 Peanut-free | Does not contain peanuts; may contain tree nuts |
| ☪ Halal | Halal-certified by a recognised Canadian certification body |
| ✡ Kosher | Kosher-certified (COR, MK, or OU) |
| 🍃 Organic | Certified organic under the Canada Organic Regime |

These tags are informational only and do not replace the ingredient list or
allergen declarations. Customers with severe allergies should always contact
the manufacturer directly for confirmation.

## 7. Customer Responsibility

Boréal Marché makes every reasonable effort to provide accurate allergen
information. However, the customer is ultimately responsible for reading the
product label and making informed decisions. Boréal Marché is not liable for
allergic reactions resulting from:

- Consumption of a product whose label was not read.
- Reliance on website filters as a sole safety measure.
- Product reformulations that occurred between purchase and consumption.
