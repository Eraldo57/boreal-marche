# Boréal Marché — Return and Refund Policy

*Last updated: 2024-11-01*

## 1. General Principles

Boréal Marché wants every customer to be satisfied with their purchase. If you
are not happy with an item, you may request a return or a refund subject to the
conditions described in this policy.

All return and refund requests must be submitted within the applicable timeframe
counted from the date of delivery (or the date of pickup for click-and-collect
orders).

## 2. Non-Perishable Products

### 2.1 Eligibility

Non-perishable products (canned goods, dry goods, household items, cleaning
supplies, personal care) may be returned if:

- The return request is made **within 30 days** of delivery.
- The item is **unopened and in its original packaging**.
- The item is **not a final-sale or clearance item** (marked "Vente finale" on the
  product page).

### 2.2 Process

1. Log in to your Boréal Marché account and navigate to "Order History."
2. Select the order and click "Request a return."
3. Choose the items you wish to return and provide a reason.
4. Print the prepaid return label that is emailed to you.
5. Ship the item(s) within 7 days.

Once we receive and inspect the returned items, a refund is processed to the
original payment method within 5 business days.

### 2.3 Return shipping cost

For non-perishable returns, the return shipping cost is borne by the customer
unless the return is due to a Boréal Marché error (wrong item shipped, item
defective on arrival). A flat return shipping fee of $7.95 is deducted from the
refund.

## 3. Perishable Products

### 3.1 General rule

Due to food-safety regulations, **perishable products cannot be physically
returned**. This includes all items tagged as "refrigerated" or "frozen," fresh
produce, bakery items, and any item with an expiration date within 14 days of
delivery.

### 3.2 Quality issues

If a perishable product arrives in unsatisfactory condition (spoiled, past its
expiry date, incorrect temperature, wrong item), you may request a refund or
replacement:

- Submit a request **within 48 hours** of delivery.
- Include **at least one photo** showing the issue.
- A refund or replacement is issued without requiring the item to be returned.

### 3.3 Taste or preference

Perishable items returned for reasons of taste or preference ("I didn't like
it," "I changed my mind") are **not eligible for a refund or exchange**. We
encourage customers to review product descriptions, ingredients, and reviews
before purchasing.

## 4. Damaged or Defective Items

Items that arrive damaged (broken packaging, crushed, leaking) or defective are
eligible for a full refund or replacement regardless of whether they are
perishable or non-perishable. Report the issue within 48 hours with a photo.

## 5. Wrong Item Received

If you receive an item you did not order, contact us within 48 hours. We will
arrange for the wrong item to be picked up at no cost and send the correct item
or issue a full refund.

## 6. Promotional and Gift Items

- **Items purchased with a promotional code** are refunded at the price actually
  paid (after the discount), not the full retail price.
- **Free promotional items** (e.g., free item with purchase) must be returned
  together with the qualifying purchase to receive a full refund on the
  qualifying item. If only the qualifying item is returned, the retail value of
  the free item is deducted from the refund.
- **Gift cards** are non-refundable and non-exchangeable.

## 7. Subscription Orders

Customers subscribed to recurring deliveries (Boréal Box) may cancel individual
upcoming deliveries up to 72 hours before the scheduled dispatch date. Deliveries
already dispatched follow the standard return rules above.

To cancel a subscription entirely, go to "My Account" → "Subscriptions" →
"Cancel." Active loyalty points earned through the subscription are retained.

## 8. Refund Methods

Refunds are issued to the original payment method:

| Payment method | Refund timeline |
|----------------|----------------|
| Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) | 5–10 business days |
| Debit card (Interac) | 5–10 business days |
| Boréal Marché gift card | Instantly re-credited |
| Loyalty points | Points restored within 24 hours |
| PayPal | 3–5 business days |
