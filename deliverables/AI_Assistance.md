# AI assistance and verification

AI tools assisted with repository inspection, Streamlit explanations and plots, API/schema
integration, the feature-minimisation implementation, real-resource Optuna pruning, uncertainty
diagnostics, tests, documentation and submission materials during November 2026. Earlier
assistant correctness judgements identify a single AI rater in their saved report.
These are not independent human evaluations.

The project owner challenged the coupon economics, equal offers at different probabilities,
metric definitions, basket naming, coverage and French answers. Those challenges are reflected
in the limitations and presentation. The owner must review the final explanation and personal
reflection before submitting them as their own work.

Verification includes executable schema/behaviour tests, saved-data metric checks, real pipeline
runs, paired customer bootstraps and visual inspection of rendered submission materials.
Passing these checks does not establish coupon uplift, contemporary performance, legal compliance
or cloud deployment. Exact execution logs and remaining external requirements are in
`reports/current_run/` and `docs/CAPSTONE_REQUIREMENTS_STATUS.md`.

The 25-feature model and outputs were archived before the 12-feature retraining. The decision to
remove features used the June development-period audit; the September snapshot was evaluated
again under the locked protocol. That reused snapshot is not a new independent holdout.

Substantial newly assisted modules cite this record. Existing source and upstream dependencies
retain their own provenance and licences; this record does not claim exclusive authorship.
