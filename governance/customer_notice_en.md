# How Boréal Marché uses automated predictions

*Plain-language notice. Boréal Marché is a fictional retailer created for a college project; this notice describes how the demonstration system works.*

**What the system does.** A computer model estimates how likely you are to shop with us again in the next 90 days. The estimate can lead to a proposal to send you an offer, such as a coupon.

**What information it uses.** Only your purchase history with us: how recently and how often you bought, how much you spent, the size and variety of your orders, your returns and cancellations, the days and times you shop, and the country of your purchases. It does not use your name, email address, payment details or any sensitive information.

**Who decides.** The model never decides alone. A member of our team reviews each proposal, approves or rejects it, and records why. Nothing is sent to you automatically.

**Your rights.** You can ask us:

- which information about you was used;
- the main reasons behind the estimate;
- to correct information that is wrong;
- for a person to review a decision, and to pass on your comments to them.

**Contact.** Our privacy officer: privacy@borealmarche.ca. Section 7 of the full privacy notice gives the details.
